It's in our name for a reason. Every gutter is hand-cleared, not just blown out — we pull debris by hand, flush every downspout, and test the flow before we call the job done.

Our Story

Built On Friendship, Backed By Fidelity

Fidelity Gutter Cleaning Services LLC started the way a lot of good things do — two friends who kept hearing the same complaint from homeowners across Greater Boston: contractors who never showed up, rushed the job, or left a mess behind. We knew gutter cleaning, roof cleaning, and power washing didn't have to feel like a gamble, so we built a company around the one thing the trade was missing — real accountability.

We've spent enough time on ladders together to trust each other with our safety, and that same trust is what we bring to your roofline. Every technician we bring onto this crew learns proper harness and ladder protocol before they ever touch a gutter, because the fastest way to lose a customer's confidence is to get hurt — or damage — their home. It's why our own shirts read "Safety is the Priority. Quality is the Standard." For us, that's not a slogan. It's a promise we make to each other, and to every homeowner who lets us on their roof.

Whether we're clearing gutters before a New England winter, soft-washing a roofline, or keeping a commercial property maintained year-round, we treat every job like it's our own home. That's the standard we hold each other to — and the one you can count on every time you call Fidelity.
